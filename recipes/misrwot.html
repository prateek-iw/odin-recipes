Misr Wot (White Guys Edition)

Description

A spicy Ehtiopian lentil dish made with onions, clarified and spiced butter, lentils, and delicious bebere spices. This aromatic, delicious dish is filling and great to share with family and friends. It's great paired with veg, rice, naan, or traditional injera (best made with pure teff).

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lentils
  • 2 tbsp. berbere spice
  • 1 onion diced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp. clarified butter (nitter kibbeh)
  • 4 cloves of garlic, diced
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Melt half of the butter in a copper or steel pot. Once melted, add chopped onions and sautee for approx. 10 minutes (until golden brown).
  2. Add chopped tomatoes, garlic, and berbere spice, cook for a further 5-7 minutes. Add water and lower heat if needed, to prevent burning.
  3. Add chicken/vegetable stock, lentils, and salt to taste. Bring to a boil, cover, and lower heat. Simmer for 40 minutes. Stir occasionally.
  4. After cooking, remove from heat, and add last half of butter and berbere spice. Stir until thoroughly mixed. Serve with quinoa, injera, naan, rice, or vegetables.
